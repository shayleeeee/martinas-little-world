My beba, I made this little world because I want you to have somewhere that feels like a hug from me — a tiny place that says, even when I can’t find the perfect words, my heart is full of you. On the easy days, on the messy days, when we’re laughing until we can’t breathe, and when life asks more of us. I want to keep showing you that my love is not only something I say — it’s something I choose to put care and effort into, again and again.
